\section{Scope}
\label{sec:apps-intro}

The applications in this chapter range from individual kernels to
integrated scientific workflows (\cref{sec:benchmark-application-categories}).
In the PC5 collaboration, the Ktirio Urban Building demonstrator
combines components from WP1, WP6 and WP7. The individual fiches
describe the case, methods and measurements for each application.
\Cref{tab:v3-application-registry,tab:v3-benchmark-registry} give the
development stages of the applications and the status of their benchmarks.
