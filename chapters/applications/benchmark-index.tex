\section{Benchmark index}
\label{sec:benchmark-index}

Each application fiche brings together its description, methods, input/output
specifications and reported benchmark results. Operational readiness and the availability of
reported results are recorded separately in the registry below; they do not by
themselves establish reproducibility. Applications still under development may
have specifications without completed benchmark results.

Application identifiers link to the corresponding fiches when their specifications
are included in this report.
Every record has exactly one application owner in this draft.  The CSV contains
the full normalized fields: owner and WPs, category and scope, repository and
commit, configuration, machine/resources, toolchain, baseline, scientific and
performance KPIs, repetitions, evidence, latest run, summary and limitations.

The reduced-basis results for \nolinkurl{app-feelpp-rb} are carried over
from D7.1 v1. Their registry stage explicitly records this origin; no new 2026
benchmark campaign is claimed.

The HPDDM/DFN results for \nolinkurl{app-hpddm-dfn} match the 2025 report
sources tagged \href{https://github.com/numpex/exa-ma-d7.1/blob/4473ef61ceec1773e7593385c92a87959438d92c/chapters/applications/specs/app-hpddm-dfn.tex}{v1.93.0}
(and v1.92.0). They are historical results, not a new 2026 campaign; they were
not present in the 2024 v1.0.0 release. This identifies the report source without
claiming verification of the executable configuration or raw data.
