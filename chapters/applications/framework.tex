\section{Software and execution environments}
\label{sec:apps-framework}

The framework index connects the software fiches to the cases that
exercise them (\cref{tab:framework-application-map}). Individual
campaigns use the environment of the corresponding implementation.
For example, the Composyx diffusion campaign uses the CUDA and HIP
drivers on two different platforms, and the Feel++ I/O campaign
provides launch configurations and performance data for its cases
(\cref{sec:app:specs:app-composyx-diffusion,sec:app:specs:app-feelpp-io}).
The evidence recorded for each campaign follows
\cref{sec:benchmark-campaign-records}.
