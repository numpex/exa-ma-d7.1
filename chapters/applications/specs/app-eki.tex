\subsection{Description of the benchmark}
The proposed WP4 mini-app applies ensemble Kalman inversion to a
simplified wave equation for geophysical parameter estimation. WP1
contributes spectral-element and discontinuous Galerkin discretizations,
and WP6 contributes uncertainty propagation. The implementation
specification uses PETSc and GeoS with ensemble members distributed via
MPI. The catalogue gives 15 January 2027 as the target.

\subsection{Benchmarking methodology}
Benchmark \texttt{BM-EKI-01} proposes verification against a manufactured
solution and weak scaling in MPI ensemble mode.

\subsection{Input/Output Dataset Description}
The specification identifies Gmsh meshes and in-house inputs and
outputs. It does not identify a versioned dataset.
