\section{Application planning and maturity}
\label{sec:app:specs:proposed}

The stages in \cref{tab:v3-application-registry} describe development.
\emph{Brainstorming} and \emph{spec-only} identify concepts and
specifications. \emph{In development} identifies an implementation
under way. \emph{Prototype-ready} indicates that a prototype is
available, and \emph{benchmark-ready} that the application can be used
for a campaign. Measurements appear separately in
\cref{tab:v3-benchmark-registry}.
