{Demonstrate weak and strong scalability of conforming constrained Delaunay triangulation for tetrahedral meshes and remeshing.}
{CGAL, Intel TBB}{brainstorming}
\subsection{Description of the benchmark}
This proposed test examines conforming constrained Delaunay
triangulation and remeshing of tetrahedral meshes using CGAL.

\subsection{Benchmarking methodology}
\texttt{BM-CGAL-CCDT3-01} would measure strong and weak scaling with
Intel TBB on a single node. The tetrahedral input series and conformity
and quality criteria have yet to be specified.
