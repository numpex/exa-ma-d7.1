\subsection{Description of the benchmark}
The proposed WP5 study uses FreeFEM++ to evaluate an iterative,
finite element method for shape optimization subject to a volume
constraint.

\subsection{Benchmarking methodology}
The initial sequential configuration would use manufactured and
analytical solutions to check the computed shape.
