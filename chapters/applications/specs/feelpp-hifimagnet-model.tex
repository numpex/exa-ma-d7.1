\subsubsection{Shared nonlinear thermoelectric forward model}
\label{sec:app:feelpp:shared-thermoelectric}
The forward model used to specify the CPU, MI300A, inverse and control cases is
steady electric conduction with Joule heating~\cite{daver2016,Hild2020}:
\begin{gather}
 \nabla\cdot j=0,\qquad j=-\sigma(T,p)\nabla V,\label{eq:feelpp:hifi:electric}\\
 -\nabla\cdot(k(T,p)\nabla T)=\sigma(T,p)|\nabla V|^2.
 \label{eq:feelpp:hifi:heat}
\end{gather}
Here $V$ is in V, $T$ in K, $j$ in A\,m$^{-2}$, electrical conductivity
$\sigma$ in S\,m$^{-1}$ and thermal conductivity $k$ in
W\,m$^{-1}$\,K$^{-1}$. The vector $p$ collects the material/cooling parameters
of the selected model. On cooling surfaces,
$-k\nabla T\cdot n=h(T-T_c)$, with $h$ in W\,m$^{-2}$\,K$^{-1}$ and coolant
reference temperature $T_c$ in K; remaining thermal surfaces are insulated
unless the input deck specifies otherwise. Electrical surfaces other than the
terminals are insulating in the baseline model. Contact/interface laws, when
used, must be supplied explicitly rather than absorbed into an unspecified
conductivity.

The \href{https://docs.feelpp.org/toolboxes/latest/thermoelectric/theory.html}{toolbox theory}
describes potential-driven terminals. To specify the imposed-total-current
HiFiMagnet variant, ground one terminal and introduce a uniform unknown
potential drop $\Delta V$ on the other, with
\[
  \int_{\Gamma_+}j\cdot n\,ds=I_\star.
\]
The current sign uses the outward normal. The exact integral constraint and
its nonlinear coupling settings must be checked in the HL-31 CFG/model files;
the existence of a generic potential-driven toolbox does not establish that
this particular current-controlled input deck is ready.

The shared validation outputs are terminal current balance, electrical power
$P=\int_\Omega\sigma|\nabla V|^2\,dx$ in W, heat balance and peak temperature.
Temperature-dependent material laws, cooling regions and coefficients,
finite-element order, nonlinear tolerances and interface conditions are part
of the case definition. For new comparisons, select a mesh-convergence
criterion and freeze it before performance measurements. A magnetic-field
observation $\mathcal B(j)$ requires a separately specified magnetic evaluation
operator; it is not an output of the conduction equations alone.
