\subsection{Description of the benchmark}
The cwipi-samurai mini-app couples two independent Samurai/PETSc solvers
(a solid and a differentially heated fluid cavity) through ONERA's Cwipi
library to simulate unsteady, nonlinear solid-fluid conjugate heat transfer.
It serves as a reference case for validating the high-order multistep coupling
scheme in Cwipi, showing temporal order k+1 up to order 4, before it is used
in an HPC context.
