\subsection{Description of the benchmark}
WP2 uses Scimba to investigate neural PDE solvers for a 3D tokamak.
The approach pairs a fluid model with a kinetic model. On the kinetic
side, the team combines neural particle methods with learned flow
maps. Coupling the two models is a goal of the demonstrator.

\subsection{Benchmarking methodology}
Benchmark \texttt{BM-SCIMBA-PLASMA-01} is proposed. The catalogue
specifies error against a manufactured solution as a verification
metric.
