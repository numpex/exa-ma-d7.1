\subsection{Description of the benchmark}
Optimal-operation application: determine coil-group currents and cooling controls that reach a target magnetic field with a field-tracking and electrical-power objective while satisfying temperature, voltage, power, total-current and actuator constraints.
\paragraph{Specification boundary.}
The output is an operating/control vector and its engineering margins, not
calibrated material parameters.  \texttt{BM-FEEL-HIFI-CTRL-01} is a proposed
verification/scalability record based on Taylor-gradient tests, target-field
error, constraint violation and KKT residual.
\paragraph{Planned contribution.} Review the deterministic objective below; select the target field, control bounds and engineering limits. Uncertainty scenarios are a separate planned extension.


\paragraph{Proposed deterministic baseline.}
Let $z$ contain coil-group currents (A) and chosen cooling controls, with their
physical units declared. Given fixed material parameters, solve the shared
forward model in \Cref{sec:app:feelpp:shared-thermoelectric} and minimize
\[
 J(z)=\frac12\|W_B(\mathcal B(j(z))-B_{\rm target})\|_2^2
       +\beta\,P(z)/P_{\rm ref},\qquad z_{\min}\le z\le z_{\max},
\]
subject to declared limits on peak temperature, terminal voltage, current and
power. $P_{\rm ref}>0$ is a normalization power and $\beta\ge0$ a stated weight.
Supply the field sampling region/operator and target, all bounds/limits and
cooling units before solving. No thermal-stress objective is claimed without
a mechanical model. This is a control problem; calibrated material parameters
belong to the separate inverse application.

\paragraph{Verification and delivery plan.}
Start with a deterministic feasible reference control. Check directional
Taylor gradients, state residuals, constraint violation, stationarity/KKT
residual and sensitivity to mesh/solver tolerances. Retain field error,
power, peak temperature, voltage/current margins, iteration history and PDE
solve count. CFG/JSON supplies the forward physics; control/adjoint/optimizer
orchestration remains planned unless a reviewed existing driver is identified.
Robust extensions require explicit uncertainty scenarios and probabilities,
which parameters vary, and an agreed risk objective; expectation or CVaR is
not silently substituted for the deterministic baseline.

\subsection{Benchmarking methodology}


\begin{description}
  \item[Method-Algorithm WP1:] nonlinear thermo-electric finite elements, coupled PDE-scalar formulation, integral total-current boundary condition
  \item[Method-Algorithm WP2:] ROM, hyper-reduction, surrogate-assisted optimization, FOM/ROM switching
  \item[Method-Algorithm WP3:] nonlinear block solvers, repeated state/adjoint solves, PETSc/HPDDM preconditioning
  \item[Method-Algorithm WP5:] PDE-constrained optimal operation and control: target-field tracking, power and thermal objectives; adjoint gradients; bound/general constraints; multiple coil-current and cooling controls; deterministic and robust formulations
  \item[Method-Algorithm WP6:] uncertain material and cooling parameters, stochastic and robust optimization, expectation and CVaR objectives
  \item[Framework:] Feel++, PETSc/TAO, HPDDM, Sage-HPC
  \item[parallel\_framework:] MPI, Kokkos, GPU - HIP
  \item[Benchmark scope:] multi-node, multi-gpu, full-system, solver-scaling, ensemble-mode
  \item[metrics:] Taylor-gradient-test, target-field-error, electrical-power, peak-temperature, constraint-violation, KKT-residual, time-to-optimum, PDE-solves-to-solution, TAO-iterations, strong-scalability, weak-scalability, scenario-throughput
\end{description}
\paragraph{Planned contribution.} Review the proposed protocol and select the case-specific tolerances and parameter values before implementation.
