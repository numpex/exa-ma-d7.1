\subsection{Description of the benchmark}
The proposed WP5 study uses a neural-network representation of a shape
to solve a volume-constrained optimization problem in Scimba. The
representation supplies a differentiable parameterization of the
boundary.

\subsection{Benchmarking methodology}
The planned GPU study would compare the numerical solution with
manufactured and analytical solutions.
