\subsection{Description of the benchmark}
WP2 proposes a Scimba demonstrator for high-dimensional physical and
parametric PDEs. A discontinuous Galerkin solver would combine a
classical discretization with learned numerical components, such as a
basis, flux or mesh mapping.

\subsection{Benchmarking methodology}
Scimba 1.4.0 includes prototypes of learned DG bases and mappings.
The proposed demonstrator has yet to specify its test problems and
scaling measurements.
