\section{Operational application registry for v3}
\label{sec:v3-application-registry}

The operational identity, ownership, WP mapping and status below were read from
the \emph{Applications} tab of the \href{https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/19v57jpek52nQV2V0tBBON5ivGCz7Bqf3Gw-fHroVHkA/edit?gid=1301884928}{Software and Demonstrators}
workbook on 23 July 2026.  The tab contained 23 non-empty IDs (rows 2--24).
The version-controlled \texttt{app-feelpp-discr-2} specification is retained as
a repository-only record pending an editorial decision.  Empty fields and
status conflicts are not completed by inference; the full reconciliation and
actions are recorded in \texttt{data/application-reconciliation-v3.csv}.
