\section{Courses, workshops and software sessions}

WP7 and HiDALGO2 ran a course on containerization, CI/CD and benchmarking in
March 2025. In November, Mark Asch taught Bayesian inversion at Inria
Makutu, and a hands-on course used Ktirio Urban Building to teach
energy simulation at building and city scales. In January 2026, WP1 and WP7
returned to the same demonstrator in a webinar on built environments.
\Cref{sec:pc5:ktirio-urban-building} describes the underlying workflow.

At the AISSAI--NumPEx HACE workshop in Toulouse in May 2026, Emmanuel Franck
(WP2) chaired a session on neural surrogates for PDEs, including
physics-informed neural networks and neural operators.

In June, Emmanuel Franck co-organised the MAJSC workshop at IRMA in
Strasbourg and taught differentiable Galerkin methods and numerical
linear algebra. The workshop combined lectures and practical sessions
on JAX and automatic differentiation, including neural methods for PDEs
and differentiable simulation with Diffrax for optimal control and
inverse problems. The programme links to the
\href{https://gitlab.math.unistra.fr/majsc2026/hands-on}{practical materials}.

In June, Exa-MA also held four software exchanges with the HPDDM/PETSc,
TRUST, FreeFEM++ and Samurai teams. The sessions addressed GPU
preconditioners, Kokkos porting, finite-element solver interfaces and
adaptive mesh refinement.

Emmanuel Franck taught an introductory JAX session at the
AISSAI--NumPEx hackathon at IDRIS in September. The introductory track
paired JAX training with hands-on scientific computing projects. The
advanced track focused on scaling JAX applications across GPUs and nodes
on Jean Zay. Rémi Imbach's
\href{https://gist.github.com/rimbach/ee711e50d892906e3574684af5b747e7}{introductory JAX notebook}
accompanies the introductory track.
