\section{Courses, workshops and software sessions}

WP7 and HiDALGO2 ran a course on containerization, CI/CD and benchmarking in
March 2025. In November, Mark Asch taught Bayesian inversion at Inria
Makutu, and a hands-on course used Ktirio Urban Building to teach
energy simulation at building and city scales. In January 2026, WP1 and WP7
returned to the same demonstrator in a webinar on built environments.
\Cref{sec:pc5:ktirio-urban-building} describes the underlying workflow.

The June 2026 MAJSC workshop in Strasbourg paired lectures on automatic
differentiation with practical work in JAX. The programme included
differentiable Galerkin methods and numerical linear algebra,
neural methods for PDEs, and optimal control and inversion with Diffrax.
The \href{https://gitlab.math.unistra.fr/majsc2026/hands-on}{practical materials}
remain available.

In June, Exa-MA also held four software exchanges with the HPDDM/PETSc,
TRUST, FreeFEM++ and Samurai teams. The sessions addressed GPU
preconditioners, Kokkos porting, finite-element solver interfaces and
adaptive mesh refinement. \Cref{tab:v3-training-initiatives} links the
programmes and recordings.
