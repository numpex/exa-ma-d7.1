\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Appendix C. Feel++ I/O campaign records}
\label{sec:appendix-feelpp-io}

The reference curves and the extended test matrix are separate datasets.
The extended matrix varies order, field shape, writer or transfer mode,
file lifetime, mesh size and rank count. This appendix records which
reference points can be used and why the full matrix is not yet a scaling
dataset. The benchmark implementation and run protocol are in
\nolinkurl{numpex/apps-feelpp/src/app-feelpp-io}.

\paragraph{Coverage snapshot, 22 November 2026 at 00:34 Paris time.}
The extended standard-placement Discoverer matrix had 59 verified cases out
of 498 planned: one was running, seven remained behind the active bundle,
421 were held, and ten had documented out-of-memory failures. The Gaya
matrix had four verified cases out of 83; the remaining 79 were held.
Standalone Gaya baselines were outside those holds. These counts exclude
the reference curves above. Unverified, deferred and failed tests do not
have a plotted timing. The native HDF5 127.52M/1,024-rank case (4520774)
remains missing; the 8,192-rank point passed.

\paragraph{Reduced-density recovery.}
Thirty-one of 65 isolated recovery cases had passed by the snapshot.
These cases preserve the partition count but reduce ranks per node and
isolate heavy fields; they are not replacements for standard-placement
timings. No DG P3 field is planned at 127.52 million cells in this
matrix: even the scalar has 2.55 billion degrees of freedom, beyond
the campaign's conservative signed 32-bit envelope. Planned DG P3
limits are 8M cells for the full tensor, 32M for the vector and 64M for
the scalar. Gaya HDF5 timings are restricted to one NFS client after
two-client readback failed; they are not pooled with Discoverer Lustre.

\paragraph{Targeted repeat tests.}
The 8.43-million-cell test at 512 ranks (4520927) rotates the order of the
three EnSight writer strategies across paired repeats, verifies payloads
against rank zero and tests independent versus collective HDF5 on the
same allocation. A paired Darshan job (4520931) is intended to check
the writer ranks and request sizes separately from the unprofiled
timings. Neither job contributes results to this snapshot. A repeated
moving-geometry export, repeated high-scale timing and the broader
order-by-writer matrix remain follow-up measurements.
