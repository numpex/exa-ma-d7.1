\section*{Executive Summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Executive Summary}
\label{sec:summary}

Exa-MA develops numerical methods and open-source software for exascale
simulation and inference within NumPEx. This edition brings together the
research advances, software releases, application benchmarks, training
and management reports. By 26 November, HAL recorded \textbf{47 research
outputs for 2026} and \textbf{77 since the project began}, including
preprints. D0.2 identifies \textbf{18 published international peer-reviewed
papers in 2026 and 37 cumulatively}, against a project target of more
than 50 (\cref{tab:exama-publications-2026}).

The research spans bounds on information gain for sensor placement,
reduced models for nonlinear flows and solvers for heterogeneous
machines. In co-design with Exa-DI and HiDALGO2, the Ktirio Urban Building
team simulated \textbf{207,624 buildings on 32\,768 MPI ranks} on Discoverer
in the \href{https://exa-ma.numpex.fr/highlights/ktirio-paris-8km.html}{Paris 8\,km case}, with field output disabled.
The GPU campaigns benchmarked Composyx domain decomposition on JUPITER
and large-scale optimisation with Exa-Zellij on LUMI-G.

Exa-MA met the four milestones due in October, covering software stack
releases, Level~1 demonstrators, mini-apps and \textbf{benchmarking at
exascale}. WP7 and the scientific teams will extend the CPU
and GPU campaigns and document the conditions needed to reproduce the
application benchmarks.
