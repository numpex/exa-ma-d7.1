\section*{Executive Summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Executive Summary}
\label{sec:summary}

The 2026 Exa-MA collection brings together the work package reports,
software fiches, application and benchmark results, and training material.
\Cref{tab:deliverable-correspondence} locates each contractual
deliverable. The management report covers the year through 18 November.
It records the recruitment decisions, the AVNIR Energy CIFRE thesis and
the revised October milestones
(\cref{sec:management-year-4}).

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{WP1, discretization.} The team implemented solid-shell
    elements for thin structures and distributed geometric queries for
    the Ktirio Urban Building demonstrator. The Ktirio-Geom case reports
    terrain-generation timings (\cref{tab:advance:WP1-A2026-2,tab:advance:WP1-A2026-3,sec:app:specs:app-feelpp-ktirio-geom}).
  \item \textbf{WP2, surrogates and scientific machine learning.}
    Scimba~1.4.0 adds differentiable classical discretizations in JAX.
    The team tested learned bases in discontinuous Galerkin examples.
    The work package also reports advances on kinetic closure and
    nonlinear reduced bases (\cref{sec:wp2-main-advances-2026}).
  \item \textbf{WP3, solvers.} HPDDM~2.4.0 and PETSc~3.25 provide a
    CUDA path for domain decomposition preconditioning. Composyx
    reached 88\% weak-scaling efficiency on 32 Jean Zay nodes with
    CUDA and 89\% on 128 Adastra nodes with HIP, each relative to its
    one-node reference (\cref{tab:advance:WP3-A2026-5,sec:app:specs:app-composyx-diffusion}).
  \item \textbf{WP4, inverse problems and data assimilation.} WP4
    tested a multi-fidelity 4D-Var preconditioner on a shallow-water
    model and derived bounds on information gain for sensor placement.
    The Feel++ interface for variational optimization passed
    functional and variational-form tests. HiFiMagnet and ensemble
    Kalman inversion have proposed benchmarks (\cref{chap:wp4}).
  \item \textbf{WP5, optimization.} On 8,000 LUMI-G GPUs, the
    Exa-Zellij variational quantum eigensolver workflow reached
    60.98\% strong-scaling efficiency. A separate fractal Bayesian
    optimization study reports about 85\% on the same number of GPUs
    (\cref{sec:wp5-main-advances-2026}).
  \item \textbf{WP6, uncertainty quantification.} The \texttt{feelstats}
    implementation provides streaming statistics and approximate quantiles
    for ensembles of Ktirio Urban Building simulations
    (\cref{tab:advance:WP6-A2026-2,sec:pc5:ktirio-urban-building}).
\end{itemize}

WP7 coordinates co-design with Exa-DI and connects the methods to
application tests. The benchmark index links the cases and records
which have reported results (\cref{chap:wp7,tab:v3-benchmark-registry}).
The training chapter includes the June JAX workshop and software
sessions (\cref{chap:training}). Earlier KPI figures are retained in
\hyperref[sec:appendix-b1]{Appendix~B.1}.
