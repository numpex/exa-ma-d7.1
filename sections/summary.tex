\section*{Executive Summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Executive Summary}
\label{sec:summary}

\noindent
\textbf{Scope.} This 2026 integration edition (v2.95.0, towards v3.0.0)
consolidates the Exa-MA deliverables in a single annual \emph{mega-deliverable}.
It brings together project management (D0.2), the scientific advances of
WP1--WP6, software frameworks, application specifications and benchmark
results, and WP7 coordination, co-design and training. The correspondence
with contractual deliverables is given in
\Cref{tab:deliverable-correspondence}; D0.1 was delivered separately.
The report connects methods \textrightarrow\ software
\textrightarrow\ mini-apps/demonstrators \textrightarrow\ metrics, with
references to code, datasets, scripts and reported results.

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Project management (D0.2).} \Cref{chap:management} adds an annual reporting
framework for project coordination, human resources, events and external
partnerships. The annual management assessments remain to be completed and
validated by the coordination team.

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Scientific contributions.} The scientific chapters present the
2026 advances alongside the retained contributions from earlier editions,
separating methodology from the implementation details in
\Cref{chap:software}. The following highlights retain the 2024--2025 context.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{WP1 Discretization.} Robust meshing and adaptation at scale, including
        feature-aware workflows and fully parallel anisotropic adaptation with ParMmg,
        plus a high-order wave proxy-app (CPU/GPU). % WP1§2.1--2.3
  \item \emph{WP2 MOR/Surrogates/SciML.} PINNs with domain decomposition, FNO-based
        warm-starts for nonlinear solves, nonlinear ROM with neural implicit representations. % WP2§3.2
  \item \emph{WP3 Solvers.} GenEO extensions to non-SPD systems; mixed-precision and
        variable-accuracy Krylov (PROMISE); fault-tolerant PCG patterns; partitioned coupling
        building blocks. % WP3§4.2
  \item \emph{WP4 Data Assimilation.} Stochastic inversion application specifications with
        manufactured tests and ensemble-MPI design. % WP4§5.3
  \item \emph{WP5 Optimization.} Fractal continuous optimization at scale; parallel Bayesian
        optimization for expensive objectives; neural level-set shape optimization. % WP5§6.2
  \item \emph{WP6 UQ.} Multi-output GP surrogates with physical constraints; graph-indexed
        kernels (SWWL) for mesh/graph outputs; categorical-kernel benchmarks. % WP6§7.2--7.5
\end{itemize}

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Applications and demonstrators.} \Cref{chap:applications} presents
applications at different stages, from conception and specification through
implementation to benchmarking. It brings together the application registry,
cross-WP mapping, benchmark index and individual fiches, including results
where available. Completed benchmarks are not required for every application
at this stage.

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Software production and benchmarking.}
\Cref{chap:methodology} describes a unified methodology: CI/CD with nightly/non-regression and ReFrame,
containerized runs (Docker/Apptainer), FAIR data/DOIs (Zenodo), energy/perf/scaling metrics,
and acceptance criteria per application tier (mini-app, extended mini-app, demonstrator).

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Coordination, co-design and training.} \Cref{chap:wp7} records
WP7 coordination and initial co-design feedback towards D7.2, including
the PC5 working groups and collaboration with the HiDALGO2 Centre of Excellence.
\Cref{chap:training} integrates D7.3 training and capacity-building material.
