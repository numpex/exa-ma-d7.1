\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

Several reported advances have reached implementation or testing.
In WP3, PETSc~3.25 exposes the CUDA path for HPDDM. Composyx has weak
scaling results for heterogeneous diffusion on Jean Zay and Adastra
(\cref{sec:HPDDM:2026,sec:app:specs:app-composyx-diffusion}). In WP2,
Scimba adds differentiable classical discretizations in JAX
(\cref{sec:wp2-main-advances-2026}). In the Ktirio Urban Building
collaboration, WP1, WP6 and WP7 contribute meshing, coupling and
statistical components to the same demonstrator
(\cref{sec:pc5:ktirio-urban-building}). The terrain benchmark measures
one part of that workflow.

The next application tests concern, among others, inverse parameter
recovery for HiFiMagnet, weak scaling of ensemble Kalman inversion,
and validation of the Ktirio coupled workflow. The FDA nozzle fiche
specifies the experimental comparisons and scaling protocol for a
future campaign. The benchmark index distinguishes these specifications
from cases with recorded measurements
(\cref{sec:wp4:benchmarks,sec:app:specs:app-feelpp-discr-2,tab:v3-benchmark-registry}).

The D0.2 report records the revised October schedule for MS4 and MS5
and the status of the October submission on 18 November
(\cref{sec:management-year-4}).
