\label{sec:conclusions}

The 2026 campaigns quantify distinct costs in large simulations.
On Discoverer, Ktirio Urban Building simulated 207,624 Paris buildings
at 32\,768 MPI ranks with field output disabled. The phase timings show
that doubling the ranks from 8\,192 to 16\,384 reduced the building-only
solar-mask time by 38\%. The I/O study measures the cost of preparing, writing
and reloading simulation outputs. Composyx and Exa-Zellij report
parallel efficiency for GPU-based domain decomposition and distributed
optimisation. These campaigns provide reference runs for measuring
how the methods behave within larger workflows
(\cref{tab:exadi-ktirio-results,tab:Feelpp:solar-mask-discoverer,sec:app:specs:app-feelpp-io,sec:app:specs:app-composyx-diffusion,sec:wp5:benchmarks}).

The 2026 campaigns, including the Composyx runs on JUPITER, met the
October benchmarking milestone. The next
application tests include measuring the coupled Ktirio workflow with
simulation output, checking parameter recovery in HiFiMagnet and running
the ensemble Kalman inversion weak-scaling benchmark. The FDA nozzle
fiche specifies comparisons against experimental data for a future
validation and scaling campaign
(\cref{tab:exama-milestones-october-2026,sec:pc5:ktirio-urban-building,sec:wp4:benchmarks,sec:app:specs:app-feelpp-discr-2}).

The partnerships reported in D0.2 extend the use of these methods.
JNL-G applies Exa-MA's multiphysics and model-reduction work to
the LNCMI magnet digital twin. The industrial doctorates bring solver,
coupling and inverse-problem methods to test cases defined with their
partners (\cref{sec:management-year-4}).
