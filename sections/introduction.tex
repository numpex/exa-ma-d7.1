\subsection{Scientific scope}
\label{sec:purpose}

This document is the 2026 edition of the Exa-MA mega-deliverable: a
single integrated report that gathers the contractual deliverables of
the 2026 reporting period, continuing the collection format of the
earlier editions. It replaces the separate deliverable PDFs of the
first project years while remaining traceable to them:
\cref{tab:deliverable-correspondence} maps every chapter to its
contractual deliverable, and the standalone work-package and software
extracts are generated from the same source. The final v3.0.0 edition
is scheduled for submission to the ANR and the Scientific Advisory
Board on 30~November~2026 after review by the work-package leads.

Exa-MA develops numerical methods and scientific software for
simulation and inference on heterogeneous supercomputers within
PEPR NumPEx. The work addresses the different sources of
computational cost in a simulation workflow: refining a discretisation
(WP1), reducing or learning surrogate models (WP2), solving the
resulting linear systems on heterogeneous machines (WP3), inferring
parameters from observations (WP4), optimising designs and controls
(WP5), and propagating uncertainty through ensembles (WP6). WP7
integrates these methods into common software, application and
benchmark portfolios and coordinates co-design across NumPEx.
This edition follows the work reported in 2026 from its numerical
formulation to the software and experiments that implement and test it.
Earlier results are kept where they provide a reference for comparison.

The mega-deliverable assembles the contractual reports for this period.
D0.2 reports the consortium's activities, publications and progress
against the October milestones (\cref{sec:management-milestones-2026}).
D7.1 describes the benchmarking methodology, application cases and
campaigns (\cref{chap:methodology,chap:applications}).
\Cref{chap:exadi-demonstrators} connects the Exa-DI demonstrators to the
Exa-MA methods, software and benchmark cases they use.
D7.3 and D7.4 cover training and WP7 coordination, respectively
(\cref{chap:training,chap:wp7}).

\subsection{Structure of the Document}
\label{sec:structure}

The scientific chapters describe the methods and point to their
implementations. The software fiches identify the versions and tested
configurations. The application fiches specify the numerical reference,
the computational protocol and the results where available. For example,
the thermal-bridge case checks finite-element solutions against ISO~10211
reference values and reports scaling on Discoverer
(\cref{sec:app:specs:app-feelpp-discr-1}).
\Cref{tab:framework-application-map,tab:v3-benchmark-registry} connect
the software, applications and benchmark campaigns. Earlier KPI
figures are kept in \hyperref[sec:appendix-b1]{Appendix~B.1}. The
appendices complete the record: they list the computing and data
storage infrastructures used in 2026, preserve the 2025 reporting
baseline and its follow-up (\hyperref[sec:appendix-b]{Appendix~B}), and
collect the Feel++ I/O campaign (\hyperref[sec:appendix-feelpp-io]{Appendix~C}).
